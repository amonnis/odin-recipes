Asian Cucumber Salad

Description

This Asian cucumber salad is incredibly fresh, crisp, and perfectly tossed in a tangy ginger and rice vinegar dressing. Taking a few minutes to salt and drain the cucumbers keeps them extra crunchy and helps them absorb all the delicious flavors.

Ingredients

  • 2 cucumbers - halved lengthwise, seeded, and sliced
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • ½ cup rice vinegar
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger root
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • 4 fresh red chili peppers, sliced

Steps

  1. Place cucumber slices in a colander and sprinkle with salt; set aside to drain.
  2. Whisk vinegar and sugar until dissolved; stir in sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and sesame seeds.
  3. Rinse salt off cucumber slices under cold water and place in a bowl.
  4. Add sliced red chili peppers and dressing; toss to coat and refrigerate.
